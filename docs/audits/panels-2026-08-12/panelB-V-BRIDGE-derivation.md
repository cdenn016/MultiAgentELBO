## 2. Second variation — the decisive item

  d²L/dε²|_0 = ½ [ tr(S²) − ½ (tr S)² ] =: ½ Φ(S).

## 5. The d = 2 bridge theorem (item 4, part III; the positive result)

Concavity/homogeneity is the whole story. sqrt(det h) is positively homogeneous of degree d/2 on the psd cone; any limit of a nearest-neighbour KL bond sum is linear in h, hence positively homogeneous of degree 1.    ½ 𝔤^{μν} h_{μν} ≥ sqrt(det h),
with equality iff 𝔤 = sqrt(det h) h^{-1}, i.e. iff 𝔤 ∈ [h].
