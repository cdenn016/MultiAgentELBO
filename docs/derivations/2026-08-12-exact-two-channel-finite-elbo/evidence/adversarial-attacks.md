# Adversarial attacks and responses

## Attack A: the replicas merely insert the answer

The construction selects a variational restriction that repeats (q_a) and (s_a) across private
and relational variables. This is not forced by a more microscopic ontology.

**Response.** Sustained as an interpretive limitation, rejected as an algebraic objection. The
generative joint is specified independently of the current recognition law, is normalized, and its
ELBO follows without exponentiating the target scalar. The theorem proves existence of an exact
representational lift, not uniqueness or physical emergence.

## Attack B: reciprocal graphs reintroduce the source-label normalization failure

The existing simultaneous source-label construction develops cycles for reciprocal shared
variables.

**Response.** Rejected for this scoped theorem. Every receiver owns independent relational copies,
and source laws are fixed in (H_n), so the global generative law is a finite product of normalized
mixtures. The attack returns if relational copies are identified with shared contemporaneous agent
states.

## Attack C: correlation between state and model is silently lost

Using only (q_a) and (s_a) may erase private state-model dependence.

**Response.** Rejected. The private recognition law is (\zeta_a), and its exact decomposition
retains (I_{\zeta_a}(K_a;M_a)). Equality with
(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{JT},h}^{\mathrm{lag},1}) is claimed only under explicit state-model mean
field.

## Attack D: arbitrary temperatures and action weights are being called exact

The project manuscripts also study coefficients that are not all one.

**Response.** Rejected because the theorem is explicitly unit temperature and unit private
coefficients. The nonunit extensions remain outside scope, with concrete mismatch formulas in the
boundary artifact.

## Attack E: finite exactness is being promoted to a continuum theorem

The surrounding research program studies base integrals and spatial fields.

**Response.** Rejected. The exact theorem uses counting measure on a finite set. Cell-volume,
edge-gradient, and curvature scalings are not included and no process law on section space is
claimed.

## Attack F: the typed observation term is being identified with the literal PIFB2 display

The derived scalar uses (-\mathbb E_{\zeta_a}\log\ell_a(o_a\mid K_a,M_a)), while the current
PIFB2 display leaves the model variable unbound or omits it. Calling these terms equal would make the
certificate depend on an unstated convention.

**Response.** Sustained against the literal-source identification and rejected as an objection to
the joint-typed theorem. The certificate names its proved object
(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{JT},h}^{\mathrm{lag},1}); the literal PIFB2 observation match is a separate
inconclusive, nondependency claim. Closure requires an author-approved repair that binds the model
variable and declares whether the intended term is joint-private or predictive-marginal.
