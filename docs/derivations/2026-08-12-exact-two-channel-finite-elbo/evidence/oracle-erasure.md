# Oracle erasure

Erase the desired PIFB2 match and retain only the declared normalized probability data. The product
joint, tied recognition family, KL expansion, mutual-information decomposition, and coordinate
invariance still follow. The result is a generic theorem about an adaptive two-channel replicated
latent model.

What disappears under erasure is the reason for tying the private and relational recognition
marginals and the interpretation of the two relational blocks as belief and generative-model
alignment. Thus PIFB2 selects the representation and gives it scientific meaning, but the desired
conclusion is not used as a premise of the algebraic proof.
