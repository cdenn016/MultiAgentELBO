# Oracle erasure

Erase every project-specific manuscript comparison and retain only the declared normalized
probability data. The product joint, tied recognition family, KL expansion, mutual-information
decomposition, joint-typed scalar, and coordinate invariance still follow. The result is a generic
theorem about an adaptive two-channel replicated latent model.

What disappears under erasure is the project-specific reason for tying the private and relational
recognition marginals and the interpretation of the two relational blocks as belief and
generative-model alignment. Those choices motivate the representation but do not supply any premise
of the algebraic proof. In particular, the theorem does not require or establish equality between
its joint-private observation expectation and the literal PIFB2 observation display.
