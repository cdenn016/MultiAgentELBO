# Participatory-interpretation investigator memo

This memo records an independent philosophy-of-science review. It governs interpretive scope; it
does not establish any mathematical or ontological claim.

## Governing stance

The primitive descriptive data are agent-indexed belief laws, agent-indexed generative mechanisms,
local frame data, information interfaces, and normalized interaction-record kernels. A population
generative law exists only after an explicit composition or normalization condition is met. A
population recognition law is a selected coupling of agent-local recognition laws. The resulting
joint measures state compatibility, evidence, correlation, and collective variational free
energy. They are not agents, observers, minds, or states assumed to be represented by a
participant.

This is a reconstruction of descriptive order, not a proof that reality contains nothing but
agents. The fixed contextual base, principal bundle, finite design, and measurable spaces remain
analyst-declared coordination scaffolds. The proposed revision is local-first but not
background-free.

## Neo-Kantian boundary

The defensible reading is agent relative, constitutive, and revisable. An agent's frame, model
evaluator, and admissible observation interface determine how a relational record acquires
inferential meaning for that agent. They are conditions of possible modeled experience, not
universal immutable forms imposed on every possible knower. This is an interpretation of the
formal architecture rather than a consequence of probability theory.

The present dichotomy at `Theory/12_philosophy.tex:62-72`, which places the Kantian role in the
fixed base, is too coarse. The fixed base remains analyst scaffolding. The constitutive role
belongs more naturally to the local frames, model presentations and evaluations, and typed access
interfaces. Kant supplies historical lineage for the constitutive reversal, not this manuscript's
multi-agent mathematics. Reichenbach's revisable coordinating principles and Cassirer's
relation-first transformation framework are closer comparisons, but neither proves that gauge
invariance is physical objectivity.

## Participatory boundary

Participation consists in reciprocal inferential dependence mediated by relational records inside
a fixed normalized model. An agent's recognition state can change in response to records or
messages produced through other agent or boundary nodes. No posterior is fed back as an argument
of the same-step generative kernel. This is compatible with the current fixed-joint prohibition
and the inference-loop discussion at `Theory/12_philosophy.tex:251-315`.

The observation/environment-node equivalence supports an operational agent-node presentation. It
does not establish that every random variable, physical object, or boundary condition is an
autonomous agent. Wheeler remains a bounded analogy. The manuscript may say that its inferential
ordering resembles a participatory proposal, while retaining that it is neither derived from nor
evidence for that proposal.

Neither \(\mathbb P_{\theta,V}\) nor \(\mathbb Q_{V,o,X}\) is a population mind or super-agent. A
meta-agent exists only after a coarse state, observation interface, evaluated generative
mechanism, recognition law, and update rule have been constructed. A full joint summary alone is
not such a construction.

## Operational falsifiers and guards

The local-access test asks whether an agent update uses only its declared state, model, incident
records, and received messages. Direct unrepresented access to a global law or full covariance
means the decentralized mechanism has not been supplied. The joint-extension test asks whether
the local generative mechanisms and record kernels actually define a normalized population law.
The coupling test uses two population joints with the same local marginals to retract any claim
that local recognition laws determine dependence.

The relational-record test requires every datum called agent accessible to pass through a typed
record or message kernel. The non-idleness test intervenes on a nontrivial interaction record while
holding local priors fixed; if no posterior or predictive law changes, the claimed relation is
inferentially idle. The representation test requires passive frame changes to preserve scalar
probabilities, KL values, and the ELBO.

Calling VFE a physical action requires a path-space functional, admissible variations, boundary
conditions, and derived equations of motion. Without these, it is a variational objective. The
revision must not claim universal agency, ontological closure, physical creation by observation,
canonical correlation from marginals, emergent global scaffolding, or consciousness.

Primary historical sources proposed for bounded comparison are Kant's *Critique of Pure Reason*,
Reichenbach's *The Theory of Relativity and A Priori Knowledge*, Cassirer's *Substance and
Function*, and Wheeler's participatory essays. Their role is attribution and scope, not proof.
