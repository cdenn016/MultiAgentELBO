# Oracle-erasure check

## Method

The erasure pass removed the affirmative search prior, every ledger state,
release status, PASS label, preferred-lift idea, hyperedge-selection
preference, physical analogy, and the premise that the desired construction
works. It then checked the target and every transitive dependency using only
the finite definitions, open-domain inequalities, and direct algebra.

## Closure after erasure

Positivity, normalization, and all proper marginals follow directly from
factorization and parity cancellation. The right inverse and immersion follow
from the derived singleton identities, not an assumed lift property. Even/odd
symmetry behavior follows from the parity character.

The VFE theorem follows from finite KL disintegration and normalized tangent
calculus. The general Fisher formula follows from expanding \(J=s+R\) with
all signs retained. The parity simplification follows from the already-derived
proper marginals and score projection. Residual positive definiteness follows
from an atomwise linear-independence argument. The center and weighted gates
follow by exact finite sums.

The record result follows after declaring the kernel as model data, checking
its normalization, and applying Bayes' rule. Nothing in the erased context
selects that kernel; the cancelling control independently confirms that
pairwise record marginals do not. The two-bit calculation and redundant
amplitude map supply the exact nonuniversality and rank boundaries.

Covered claims are target, positive-normalized-lift, all-proper-marginals,
symmetry-boundary, right-inverse-immersion, fixed-outside-vfe,
joint-fisher-decomposition, parity-residual-positive, center-fisher,

orthogonality, residual definiteness, VFE equality, posterior recovery, or
lift noncanonicity. Each is derived. The kernel declaration supplies data, not
the conclusion that it follows from pairwise sections. No paraphrase of the
desired physical or canonical conclusion appears as an assumption.

shows only that the search prior was unnecessary; it is not itself a proof.
