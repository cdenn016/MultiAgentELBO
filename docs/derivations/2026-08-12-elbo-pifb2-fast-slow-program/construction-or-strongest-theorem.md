# Construction or strongest theorem

For every fixed finite \(N\) and finite nonempty source set, let
\(\pi_i=(\pi_{ij})_j\) and \(\beta_i=(\beta_{ij})_j\) be strictly positive probability rows with
\(\sum_j\pi_{ij}=\sum_j\beta_{ij}=1\). Assume that \(q_i^{n+1}\) and every transported source
\((\Omega_{ij}^n)_\#q_j^n\) are normalized laws and satisfy the common-domination convention
recorded in the contract. Then the conditional source-label model

\[
P_i^n(dj,dx)=\pi_{ij}(\Omega_{ij}^n)_\#q_j^n(dx)
\]

is a normalized law. Its recognition law
\(Q_i^{n+1}(dj,dx)=\beta_{ij}q_i^{n+1}(dx)\) is also normalized and satisfies exactly

[
D_{\rm KL}(Q_i^{n+1}\Vert P_i^n)=D_{\rm KL}(\beta_i\Vert\pi_i)
+\sum_j\beta_{ij}D_{\rm KL}(q_i^{n+1}\Vert(\Omega_{ij}^n)_\#q_j^n).
]

Separately, exact profiling of the state VFE over an unrestricted fast recognition law yields the
negative log evidence conditional on the slow variables. Every compact linear gauge group admits a
positive invariant fiber metric by Haar averaging and can therefore be treated in an orthogonal
representation.

These results establish an exact finite-(N), lagged-dynamical starting point. They do not establish
same-time reciprocal emergence, adiabatic elimination, a vanishing PIFB operator residual, a
continuum process law, or a normalized coercive full-(GL(K)) theory.
